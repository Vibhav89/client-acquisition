Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy describes how Client Radar / Client Acquisition Engine ("we", "our", or "the service") handles information when you use this application. This is a product disclosure draft and must be reviewed against the final hosting, provider and legal configuration before public launch.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, the service may process your account email and authentication session; profile details such as your name, headline, biography, skills, work preferences, portfolio and service information; resume or other documents you upload; saved opportunities; proposal drafts; approval decisions; and information you enter into the product.

2. How information is used

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account session.
  • To save your profile, documents, opportunities, proposal drafts and workflow state.
  • To match opportunities against your stated skills and preferences and identify potential risk signals.
  • To generate a proposal draft when you explicitly choose the AI proposal feature.
  • To maintain application security, troubleshoot errors and operate the service.
  • If advertising is enabled, to display sponsored Native Banner placements after you accept optional advertising.

3. Storage and service providers

When Supabase authentication and persistence are configured, account and workspace data are handled through the configured Supabase project. The application uses request-scoped user authentication and database access controls. If the app is run in its local development configuration, some data may instead be held in temporary in-memory storage and may not persist after the server stops.

If you use the AI proposal feature, the opportunity details and relevant profile evidence needed to draft the proposal are sent from the application server to the AI provider configured by the operator. That provider may be OpenRouter or another OpenAI-compatible provider. Do not use the AI feature with information you are not permitted to share with that provider. The provider's own privacy and retention terms also apply.

The service may retrieve publicly available opportunity information from configured source websites or APIs. Those sources receive ordinary network request information such as IP address and request metadata when accessed.

4. Advertising and optional technologies

Advertising is disabled by default in the source configuration. If the operator enables Adsterra Native Banner ads and supplies publisher ad tags, the application asks you to accept or reject optional ads before loading the ad iframe. If you reject, the application does not render those ad placements. You can reopen Ad preferences from the application footer to change your choice.

If you accept optional ads, Adsterra and its advertising partners may receive information such as IP address, device and browser details, page or placement information, and may use cookies or similar technologies, subject to their current configuration and policies. Their processing is governed by their own privacy disclosures and settings. Review the provider's current terms before enabling ads. Essential browser storage may also be used to remember your advertising choice and authentication state.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your profile or proposal content as a product. Information may be processed by the service providers described above to provide the features you request, or disclosed where required to protect the service, comply with law, or respond to valid legal requests. Advertising providers may independently collect information through their ad technology when you consent to optional advertising.

6. External actions and user control

Proposal generation creates a draft for your review. The application is designed so that approving a draft does not itself submit an application or send a message to an employer or client. You control whether to take external actions.

7. Security and retention

We use technical controls intended to protect account and workspace information, including authenticated requests and user-scoped database access when Supabase is configured. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Data retention depends on the configured hosting, database, AI and advertising providers and on how long your account and records remain active.

8. Your choices and rights

You may choose not to use optional AI proposal generation. You may reject optional advertising and change that preference using the Ad preferences control in the application footer. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing. Contact the service operator using the contact details published on the product website to make a privacy request.

9. Children

This service is intended for people who can legally use online work and professional networking services in their jurisdiction. It is not designed for children.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the product's providers and features change. A monitored privacy contact email and the responsible operator's identity must be published before launch.
